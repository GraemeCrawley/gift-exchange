How it works

  1. Add everyone's names. Put couples or people who live together in a household so they don't draw each other.
  2. Press Draw names.
  3. Post the group link in your family chat. Each person taps their own name, saves their personal link, and adds a wish list.

Exchanges are deleted automatically 60 days after the exchange date (or one year after they're created if no date is set).
